### Brand Style Guidance for SELLWERK Designs
 
Please ensure all designs align closely with the official SELLWERK brand identity as seen on https://sellwerk.de/. The goal is to maintain a professional, trustworthy, modern, and approachable look that reflects our focus on supporting small and medium-sized businesses (Mittelstand) in digital marketing.
 
#### Primary Colors
 - *Main Brand Color (Teal/Green)*: #00AF9C (Persian Green)  
   Use this as the primary accent color for buttons, calls-to-action (CTAs), links, icons, highlights, and key elements like the logo accent or headers. It's vibrant yet professional and evokes trust and growth.
 - *Light Teal/Secondary*: #B2DFDB  
   Use for backgrounds, subtle accents, hover states, or lighter sections to add depth without overwhelming.
 
- *Neutrals*:
   - White (#FFFFFF): Primary background color for clean, open layouts.
   - Dark Gray/Black (#000000 or near-black): For body text and headings to ensure high readability.
   - Light Grays: Use soft grays for secondary text, borders, or dividers (e.g., #F5F5F5 or similar light tones observed in sections).
 
Avoid introducing new dominant colors—stick to this teal-based palette with plenty of white space for a fresh, modern feel.
 
#### Logo Usage
 - Use the official SELLWERK logo in its standard form (typically "SELLWERK" in bold uppercase lettering with the teal #00AF9C color).
 - Preferred version: Full color on white or light backgrounds.
 - Do not alter proportions, colors, or add effects unless specified.
 - Include the tagline "Mittelstand" or similar if relevant, as seen in some variants.
 
 
 
 
 
#### Typography
 - *Headings*: Bold, sans-serif font (similar to a modern font like Montserrat, Roboto Bold, or Arial Black—clean and geometric).
 - *Body Text*: Clean sans-serif (e.g., similar to Open Sans, Helvetica, or Arial) for excellent readability.
 - Use hierarchy: Large bold headings for sections, medium weights for subheadings, regular for paragraphs.
 - Color: Dark gray/black for text on white/light backgrounds; white text on teal accents if needed.
 - Keep line spacing generous for an airy feel.
 
#### Overall Style and Layout
 - *Tone*: Professional yet friendly and accessible—targeted at German Mittelstand businesses. Confident, reliable, no overly playful elements.
 - *Layout*: Clean, modern, minimalist with ample white space. Grid-based, full-width sections, hero banners with overlays or images.
 - *Elements*:
   - Buttons: Rounded or softly cornered, teal background (#00AF9C) with white text, hover to darker teal.
   - Icons: Simple line icons in teal or gray.
   - Imagery: High-quality photos of real businesses, people, offices, or digital devices (as seen in mockups). Avoid stocky generics; prefer authentic, positive scenes.
   - Sections: Alternating backgrounds (white and light teal/gray), clear CTAs like "Jetzt beraten lassen" in prominent buttons.
 
Reference the website https://sellwerk.de/ directly for examples of current homepage, buttons, forms, and sections. All designs should feel like a natural extension of the site—consistent, trustworthy, and optimized for conversions.
